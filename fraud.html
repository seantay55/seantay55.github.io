AI Fraud Detection Concept — CommBank

ContextRMIT Business Information Systems
ToolsUXPilot, Claude AI
CompletedApril 2026

Explored how generative AI could transform the way CommBank communicates fraud alerts — turning generic text notifications into personalised, conversational experiences that actually get customers to act.

The Problem

Generic alerts aren’t working

CommBank processes around 20 million transactions daily, but its fraud alerts were generic — hard to interpret, easy to ignore. With Australian bank fraud losses up 12% year-on-year, the issue wasn’t detection. It was communication. The goal: increase customer response rates to fraud alerts by 30% within 12 months.

The Gap

Existing ML systems detect fraud accurately, but the alerts they trigger are too vague for customers to act on confidently.

The Opportunity

78% of banking customers prefer conversational AI. Generative AI can turn a cold alert into a clear, personalised explanation.

The Solution

A generative AI-powered fraud assistant, embedded in the CommBank app, that explains suspicious activity and guides the customer to act.

The Mockups

How it works, screen by screen

These mockups were created using UXPilot to visualise the proposed solution. They show the full customer journey — from the initial alert through to a resolved fraud case.

01
Customer initiates contact

The customer asks Ceba — CommBank’s existing virtual assistant — about a suspicious transfer. This entry point integrates naturally into the existing app experience, requiring no new interface for customers to learn.

02
AI surfaces a personalised alert

The generative AI layer converts the ML fraud signal into a clear, specific explanation — not “suspicious activity detected”, but “this $500 transfer to a new payee matches patterns seen in recent scam reports.” The customer can block or approve in one tap.

03
Clear recommended action

For flagged transactions, the AI provides an unambiguous recommended action with transaction details surfaced inline. No navigating to a separate page — the customer can block the transaction or confirm it’s legitimate directly within the chat.

04
Automated follow-through

Once the customer acts, the AI integrates with CommBank’s fraud management systems to process the request, generate a reference ID, and create a support case — all without the customer needing to call a helpline.

Reflection

What I learned

This project pushed me to think beyond the technology — the real challenge was framing AI as a communication tool, not just a detection engine. Designing the mockups forced me to consider how a customer under stress would interpret each screen, which shaped decisions like the unambiguous “Yes, Block Transfer” button and the plain-language alert text. I also developed a clearer understanding of how AI governance, stakeholder management, and regulatory compliance intersect in a highly regulated industry like banking.
